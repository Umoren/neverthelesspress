# Learnings from The Friend Jesus Edit

This file captures the editing lessons from the first full draft pass on `The Friend Jesus`.

Use it before drafting or editing the next Nevertheless Press issue so the same prose problems do not repeat.

## 1. Remove synthetic interiority

Do not make a feeling speak for the person.

Bad pattern:

- `that quiet ache that says nobody really came close`
- `the silence that reminds you`
- `the part of you that knows`

Why it fails:

- It simulates inner life instead of showing one.
- It turns emotion into atmosphere.
- It sounds deep before it becomes clear.

Better move:

- Name the fact.
- Show the scene.
- Keep the human meaning.

Example:

`You can laugh with people all day and still go home feeling like nobody really knows you.`

## 2. Stop leaning on contrast scaffolding

The draft repeated too many `not X, but Y`, `not only X`, and `does not mean X` turns.

Use contrast only when the theology truly needs it. Otherwise, state the point directly.

Bad pattern:

`This is not X. It is Y.`

Better move:

`This is Y.`

## 3. Clarity is the core

If a sentence makes the reader decode the wording before they understand the point, rewrite it.

Example from the edit:

`Silence was not all he gave people.`

Problem:

The meaning is understandable after a pause, but the wording delays the point.

Better:

`He spoke to them.`

## 4. Complete the idea inside the sentence

Do not abandon verbs that imply an object.

Bad pattern:

`He kept revealing himself. He kept making himself known. He spoke.`

Question the reader has to answer:

To whom?

Better:

`He kept revealing himself to people. He kept making himself known to them. He spoke to them.`

## 5. Use common Bible language

Avoid private shorthand when readers already have a common name for the idea.

Fixes from the edit:

- `Eden` became `the Garden of Eden`.
- `the story` became `the Bible`, `Scripture`, or `the life of Jesus`.
- `sin entered the story` became `sin entered the world`.

Rule:

Use the name people naturally associate with the thing.

## 6. Stay present and immediate

Samuel's prose works best when the tense feels close to the reader.

Fix from the edit:

`I am not writing that from a distance.`

became:

`I am not saying that from a distance.`

The second line feels more present and direct.

## 7. State positive behavior directly

Do not define a virtue by pairing it with the negative opposite.

Bad pattern:

`kindness without an angle`

Problem:

It still depends on the negative thing to explain the positive thing.

Better:

`noticing people, making room for them, and treating them with steady kindness`

## 8. Keep theology tied to scene, Scripture, and interpretation

The strongest edits made theology come out of the biblical scene.

Example:

`When Adam and Eve hid, it showed that sin produces guilt and distance.`

The scene carries the point before the interpretation arrives.

## 9. Use personal pain without melodrama

The personal note worked because it was direct:

- long-term loneliness
- fresh breakup
- job loss
- other unwanted circumstances
- writing from pain and discovery

Rule:

Name the real context, then move back into the argument.

## 10. Preserve magazine prose discipline

For the next issue, use this pass order:

1. Structure and thesis
2. Synthetic interiority removal
3. Contrast-crutch removal
4. Rhythm and sentence-shape pass
5. Clarity-first pass
6. Present-tense pass
7. Bible common-language pass
8. Positive-behavior pass
9. Final theological coherence pass
